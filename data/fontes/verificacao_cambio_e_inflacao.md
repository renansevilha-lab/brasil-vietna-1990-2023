## Corrections that matter

1. **C3 — regime in 2008.** The IMF did not class Vietnam as stabilized or crawl-like in 2008: conventional peg (2007) → "other managed arrangement" (2008) → "stabilized" from 1 Jan 2009 → crawl-like from mid-2023. The 2012 report labels the de jure regime "floating"; recent ones say "managed floating".
2. **C7 — Chinn-Ito points the "wrong" way.** Since 2015 the index scores Vietnam (−0.17) as more open than Brazil (−1.25); in 2008 Brazil was higher (0.334 vs −0.171). Do not use it as evidence that Brazil's capital account is freer without explaining what the index measures (de jure AREAER dummies).
3. **B5 — import cover.** 3.0 months (2007) and 3.8 (2008), not ~4 in both; 1.4 months at end-2010 and 1.3 at end-2011.
4. **A5 — food weight.** 42.85% in the basket used in 2008, not ~40% (39.93% applies only from Nov 2009).
5. **A8 — credit growth.** IMF figures are 39.6% (2009) and 32.4% (2010); "US$8bn" is a press headline, the IMF measures the package at about 5% of GDP.
6. **B1 — 9.3% vs 8.5%.** Same move; the IMF uses 8.5%.
7. **B6 — dollar deposit cap.** First cap for individuals was 3% (Apr 2011), 2% only from June 2011. The gold bar monopoly ended in 2025; write it in the past tense.
8. **C6 — 49% cap.** Decree 60/2015 is no longer in force (Decree 155/2020 since 1 Jan 2021); 49% is not the current general limit.
9. **A3 — US$10bn.** Primary sources support a US$9.5bn rise in reserves and insufficient sterilisation, not a stated purchase amount.
10. **A7 — band.** The ±0.75% starting point was set in Dec 2007, not in 2008.
11. **D2/D3.** Article VIII acceptance date is 16 Nov 1999; Res. 2.689 is revoked (now Resolução Conjunta 13/2024).
